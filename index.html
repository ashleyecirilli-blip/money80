80

Women drive an estimated 70–80% of consumer purchasing decisions, a figure widely cited across marketing research.

She makes most of the money decisions. She deserves to feel confident making them.

Women run the day-to-day of household money, yet the conversations about building wealth have too often happened without them. Money 80 is a place to change that: approachable, encouraging, and built around small actions you can repeat.

The numbers tell us what she’s doing. Her goals tell us why it matters.

How it works

A practice with a rhythm, so you always know what to do next.

Every payday · about 5 minutes

Payday Reset

See what came in, what’s already spoken for, and what’s yours to direct. Plan a few small moves toward your goals, then go make them with your own bank.

No bank syncing. Typing it in is part of noticing it.

Once a week · 30 seconds to 3 minutes

Weekly Check-In

How confident do you feel? How has money felt this week? Two steady questions, plus a rotating reflection when you want one. It’s private, and it shapes how Money 80 talks to you.

No streaks. No guilt. Nothing locks if you skip.

Whenever it helps

Learn & Connect

Short reads that show up right when they’re relevant, like the emergency-fund guideline while you set one up. And a community of women who get it.

Teach when it’s relevant. Celebrate when it’s earned.

The moment that matters

Planning is good. Doing is what changes things.

Once you’ve planned your moves, Money 80 says “Amazing. Let’s make those moves!” and hands you a short checklist. You make each transfer or payment yourself, then mark it done. Only confirmed moves raise your tide, so your progress is always real.

A $10 move is a win here. We celebrate the follow-through, not the dollar amount.

Your Financial Flow

Three tides, rising on their own.

Work on one, or all three at once. Each rises as you follow through, and your milestones stay yours. A tough month can lower the water, but it never erases the high-water mark you reached.

Debt

Find your footing

Every extra payment shortens the road. No debt? This tide starts full, and you’re already on solid ground.

Emergency fund

Build your cushion

A safety net that lets you breathe. Don’t know your number yet? We’ll help you estimate it, or you can start without it.

Future & extras

Make more possible

Investing, a trip, a move, a dream with a name. The things that make the rest of it worth it.

Community

Share the action, never the balance.

Our community is money-agnostic by design. Nobody posts balances or compares numbers. We celebrate habits, lessons and brave little firsts, in four spaces: The Wins, The Ask, The Learning Table and The Check-In.

The kinds of wins you’ll share here

“I made my first transfer to my emergency fund.”

“I finally looked at my credit card statement without avoiding it.”

“I understand where my money is going for the first time.”

Privacy

Yours, and only yours.

No bank syncing

We never ask for your bank login or account numbers. You stay in control of every number.

Private by default

Your goals, numbers and reflections are never shown to anyone. Sharing only happens when you choose to post.

Hide my numbers

One tap hides every dollar amount, for checking in on the couch or on the train.

Your data isn’t for sale

Money 80 doesn’t sell or hand off your information to lenders, banks or anyone else.

Our story

Built by someone who has sat on every side of the money conversation.

Hi, I’m Ashley Cirilli. I’ve spent 24 years in personal finance: in credit unions, running financial and tax practices, managing banking relationships, and leading teams in mortgage lending. Across all of it, I kept meeting capable women who had been left out of the confidence part.

Money 80 is what I wish every one of them had: a calm, encouraging place to practice, one small move at a time.

By day, I’m VP of Marketing & Training at Hall Financial. Money 80 is my own independent project. It isn’t affiliated with, sponsored by or connected to Hall Financial, and member information is never shared with it.
